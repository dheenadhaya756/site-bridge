    Frequently Asked Questions

    Answers to common questions about Site Bridge.

    What does "agentic website" mean?

    Your site gains an AI agent that understands all of its content and can operate the interface: open pages, highlight things, fill forms. AI tools can also connect to it through an MCP server.

    Do I need to change my website's code?

    No. Scanning and the live demo need nothing from you. Going live is a single script tag pasted before the closing body tag.

    Will the agent use my brand colours?

    Yes. It detects your primary button and link colour and your font from the page itself. You can also set a colour explicitly on the script tag.

    Can the agent submit forms or make purchases?

    It fills fields with details the visitor gives, then asks for confirmation before any submit, purchase or payment. In the demo, forms are never actually sent.

    What is the MCP server for?

    It lets AI assistants like Claude, Cursor and others search your site, read pages, list offerings and follow your tasks, so your business shows up correctly inside AI tools.

    Which websites work best?

    Public marketing, product, service and e-commerce sites. Pages behind logins aren't crawled, and very JavaScript-heavy sites need the optional headless browser.
